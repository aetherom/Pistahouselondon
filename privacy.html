Form submissions and site content are stored in the restaurant's private Google account (Google Sheets / Drive via Google Apps Script). Third-party delivery platforms (Uber Eats, Just Eat, Deliveroo) and Google Maps have their own privacy policies when you interact with them.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted — unless a booking or order means we need them longer (e.g., for accounting records, kept as required by UK law).
