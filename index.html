Our story

A Taste of Hyderabad,
on Barking Road

Pista House London brings the legendary flavours of Hyderabad to East Ham. Every biryani is layered by hand and sealed under dough, steaming slowly in its own aromas — the true dum method, done the traditional way.

Alongside the biryani you'll find smoky sizzling grills straight from the clay oven, rich curries, fresh-baked breads and classic desserts — all made in-house, every day.

FAQs

Do I need to book a table?

Walk-ins are welcome, but reservations are recommended — particularly Friday to Sunday. Call 020 8821 9061 to book.

Is the meat Halal?

Yes — all our meat is Halal. Ask our team with any specific questions about preparation.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes — vegetable biryani, paneer butter masala, dal tadka and more. Look for the green dot on the menu.

Can I order takeaway or delivery?

Yes — call us for collection, or use the Order Online section to find us on Uber Eats, Just Eat and Deliveroo.

Allergen & Dietary Info

Our dishes are prepared fresh in a single kitchen that handles all 14 major UK allergens. While we take great care, we cannot guarantee any dish is completely free of traces.

Common allergens in our food
  • Gluten — naan, breads, battered starters
  • Dairy (milk) — ghee, butter, cream, paneer, kulfi
  • Nuts — korma & some desserts (cashew, almond)
  • Mustard & sesame — marinades, chutneys
  • Egg — egg biryani and some desserts

Before ordering, please tell our team about any allergies — call 020 8821 9061 so we can advise you safely.
